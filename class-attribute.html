For a one-year period from November 2010, the Marugame Genichiro-Inokuma Meseum of Contemporary Art (MIMOCA) will host a cycle of four Hiroshi Sugimoto exhibitions.

Each will showcase works by the artist thematically contextualised under the headings "Science," "Architecture." "History" and "Religion" so as to present a comprehensive panorama of the artist's oeuvre.

Hours: 10:10 - 18:00 (No admittance after 17:30)
